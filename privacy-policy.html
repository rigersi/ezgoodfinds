Privacy Policy

How EZ Good Finds handles information used through our social-media publishing integrations.

About EZ Good Finds

EZ Good Finds is a technology content and media brand. This privacy policy explains how information is handled when EZ Good Finds uses third-party platform integrations, including Pinterest.

Pinterest Integration

EZ Good Finds uses Pinterest API access to authenticate and manage its own Pinterest business account, including creating, scheduling and managing Pins through a self-hosted Postiz instance.

Data We Use

Information accessed through Pinterest is limited to what is necessary to authenticate the EZ Good Finds account and perform authorised publishing and account-management actions.

How Data Is Used

Pinterest data is used only for operating the publishing integration. EZ Good Finds does not sell, rent, scrape, profile or commercially redistribute Pinterest user data.

Artificial Intelligence

Pinterest user data obtained through the integration is not used to train artificial intelligence models.

Authentication & Security

Authentication credentials and access tokens are used only for operating authorised integrations and are not intentionally shared with unrelated third parties.

Data Retention

If an integration is disconnected or discontinued, related access credentials may be revoked or deleted when they are no longer required.
