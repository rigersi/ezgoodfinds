Privacy Policy

This policy explains how EZ Good Finds handles information in connection with our content, publishing tools and social-media integrations.

Effective September 5, 2026
EZ Good Finds
01 — Overview

About EZ Good Finds

EZ Good Finds is a technology-focused content and media brand that creates and publishes technology-related content across social-media platforms.

This Privacy Policy describes how information may be handled when EZ Good Finds uses third-party platforms, APIs and publishing integrations to manage its own social-media accounts and content.

02 — Information

Information We May Process

When a social-media account is connected to an authorised integration, EZ Good Finds may process limited information required for authentication and publishing functionality.

Depending on the platform, this may include:

  • account or channel identifiers;
  • authentication tokens and authorisation data;
  • account information provided by the connected platform;
  • content created or selected for publishing;
  • publishing status and information required to create, schedule, edit or manage posts.

EZ Good Finds does not intentionally collect unnecessary private information about users of social-media platforms.

03 — Integrations

Social-Media Integrations

EZ Good Finds may connect its own social-media accounts to authorised APIs or publishing tools in order to create, schedule and manage content.

Pinterest Pinterest API access may be used to authenticate the EZ Good Finds business account and create, schedule or manage Pins.
Meta Platforms EZ Good Finds may connect authorised Facebook, Instagram or Threads accounts for publishing and account-management purposes.
YouTube Google or YouTube APIs may be used for authorised publishing and management of the EZ Good Finds YouTube channel.
TikTok TikTok integrations may be used to authenticate and publish approved content to the EZ Good Finds TikTok account.
X X API access may be used for authorised publishing and management of content belonging to EZ Good Finds.
Reddit Reddit API access may be used to authenticate the EZ Good Finds account and submit or manage content where permitted.

Platforms listed above may be connected at different times. Listing a platform here does not necessarily mean that an integration is currently active.

04 — Pinterest

Pinterest API Usage

EZ Good Finds may use Pinterest API access to authenticate and manage its own Pinterest business account, including creating, scheduling and managing Pins through authorised publishing software.

Pinterest information obtained through this integration is used only as reasonably necessary to operate the authorised publishing workflow.

EZ Good Finds does not sell, rent, scrape, profile or commercially redistribute Pinterest user data obtained through the API.

05 — Purpose

How Information Is Used

Information obtained through authorised integrations may be used to:

  • authenticate connected EZ Good Finds accounts;
  • create and publish content;
  • schedule content for future publication;
  • edit or manage content where supported;
  • confirm whether publication succeeded or failed;
  • maintain the technical operation and security of the publishing workflow.
06 — Sharing

Data Sharing & Sale

EZ Good Finds does not sell personal information or social-platform user data obtained through authorised API integrations.

Information may be transmitted to the relevant platform or infrastructure provider when technically necessary to perform an action that has been authorised, such as authentication or publishing.

07 — Automation

Publishing Software

EZ Good Finds may use self-hosted publishing and automation software, including Postiz, to manage authorised social-media integrations.

These systems are intended to help prepare, schedule and publish EZ Good Finds content across supported platforms.

08 — Artificial Intelligence

AI & Platform Data

Social-platform user data obtained through authentication APIs is not used by EZ Good Finds to train artificial-intelligence models.

EZ Good Finds may use automation or artificial-intelligence tools in its broader content-production process, but this does not mean private social-platform user data obtained through account integrations is used for AI model training.

09 — Security

Authentication & Security

Access tokens, authentication credentials and similar information are intended to be used only for authorised integrations and account operations.

Reasonable technical measures are used to limit unauthorised access to integration credentials and associated systems.

10 — Retention

Data Retention

Authentication information and integration data may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to maintain an authorised connection or publishing workflow.

If an account or integration is disconnected, related access credentials may be revoked, invalidated or deleted when they are no longer required.

11 — External Links

Affiliate & Third-Party Links

EZ Good Finds content may occasionally contain links to third-party websites, products, services or retailers.

Some links may be affiliate links. If a purchase is made through an eligible affiliate link, EZ Good Finds may receive a commission without necessarily increasing the price paid by the visitor.

Third-party websites and services operate under their own privacy practices and policies.

12 — Hosting

This Privacy Policy Page

This page is provided as a public information page for EZ Good Finds.

The page itself does not contain a user account system, advertising tracker or data-entry form operated by EZ Good Finds.

The hosting provider may process ordinary technical information required to serve the webpage under its own terms and privacy policy.

13 — Updates

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as EZ Good Finds adds, removes or changes services, integrations or publishing tools.

When material changes are made, the effective date displayed on this page may be updated.
